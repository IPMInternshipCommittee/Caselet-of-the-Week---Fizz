Why Did Beverage Revenue Decline?

This caselet works through a beverage company's revenue decline using a simple four-step framework: understand the problem, locate it, diagnose the cause, and recommend a fix.

Not every area needs equal attention. Focus first on where the problem actually lies.

Understand the Problem → Identify Where It Exists → Understand Why It Happened → Solve the Problem
  • Where is the problem? Identify the specific business area driving the issue.
  • Why is it happening? Use data to uncover the root cause rather than relying on assumptions.
  • How should it be solved? Recommend solutions that address both the immediate issue and the underlying cause.
